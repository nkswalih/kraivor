# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders of the Kraivor community pledge to make participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

- **Be respectful and professional** — value diverse opinions and perspectives
- **Use inclusive language** — be mindful of how your words may be received
- **Give and accept constructive feedback** — critique ideas, not people
- **Support new contributors** — everyone starts somewhere
- **Focus on what benefits the community** — prioritize collective growth over individual wins
- **Collaborate openly** — share knowledge, ask questions, and help others

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind
- Hate speech, slurs, or abusive language
- Personal attacks, trolling, or intentionally disruptive behavior
- Sharing private information (doxxing) without explicit permission
- Malicious code, security exploitation, or abuse of project infrastructure
- Spam, phishing, or any deceptive activity
- Sexual content or unwelcome advances in any community space

---

## Security & Responsible Disclosure

If you discover a security vulnerability in Kraivor:

- **Do NOT** open a public GitHub issue
- **Do** report it privately by emailing the maintainers
- **Do** provide a clear description and reproduction steps
- **Do** allow maintainers reasonable time to investigate and patch before public disclosure

Violations of security reporting guidelines may result in immediate removal from the community.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards. They have the right and responsibility to:

- Remove, edit, or reject content that violates this Code of Conduct
- Temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviors they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful
- Communicate enforcement decisions clearly and fairly

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces — including GitHub repositories, Discord, X/Twitter, email, and in-person events — as well as any public representation of the project.

---

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the maintainers **privately**:

- **Email**: [conduct@kraivor.dev](mailto:conduct@kraivor.dev)

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Your identity will be kept confidential.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers will follow these guidelines when determining consequences for violations:

### 1. Correction
**Community Impact**: Minor violation (e.g., inappropriate language).  
**Consequence**: Private warning with explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate.

### 2. Warning
**Community Impact**: A pattern of minor violations or a single moderate violation.  
**Consequence**: Warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved for a specified period.

### 3. Temporary Ban
**Community Impact**: Serious violation of community standards (e.g., sustained harassment).  
**Consequence**: Temporary ban from all community interaction for a specified period.

### 4. Permanent Ban
**Community Impact**: Severe or sustained violation (e.g., hate speech, doxxing, security abuse).  
**Consequence**: Permanent ban from all community interaction.
